#### 🧰 Apps

- **[Cirrus](https://github.com/ZephyrSai/Cirrus)**: a Finder for iCloud Drive and iCloud Photos on Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad. OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, Nextcloud, WebDAV, S3 and Immich can sit beside it. Open source (GPL-3.0). [Site](https://zephyrsai.github.io/Cirrus/)
- **[EQBar](https://github.com/ZephyrSai/EQBar)**: a lean, native macOS menu bar equalizer with AutoEQ, spatial audio, per-app volume and Audio Unit plugins. [Site](https://zephyrsai.github.io/EQBar/)
- **[LeanVPN](https://github.com/ZephyrSai/LeanVPN)** and **[LeanVPN for Android](https://github.com/ZephyrSai/LeanVPN-Android)**: tiny, unofficial Proton VPN clients. A menu bar app on the Mac and just a Quick Settings tile on Android, both picking the fastest server that isn't overloaded.
- **[Port Network Studio](https://github.com/ZephyrSai/network-studio)**: an offline-capable network diagram studio in the browser, with Visio-style editing, auto-routing, IP/VLAN planning, CCTV/PoE and single-point-of-failure analysis, and Visio and draw.io export. [Open it](https://zephyrsai.github.io/network-studio/)
